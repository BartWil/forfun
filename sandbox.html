Gait Lab

Healthy walking is a chain of finely-timed events — and when one link weakens, the body improvises. Switch on a deficit and watch how the walk changes: the ghost is typical gait, the solid figure is the same person coping with the problem. The classic compensations here are exactly what clinicians look for in a gait exam.

A teaching abstraction in the sagittal (side-on) plane: the primary deficit and its main compensations are modelled on the walking kinematics; side-to-side compensations (circumduction, pelvic drop) are described but can't be shown from this angle. Grounded in Perry & Burnfield, Gait Analysis: Normal and Pathological Function (2nd ed.). Not a diagnostic tool.
