The Forge

No chart axes here. Ground reaction force is launched as a real particle spray, simulated by Rapier2D, a rigid-body physics engine written in Rust and run in your browser as WebAssembly. A bigger force literally throws matter further under real gravity and drag. Muscle activation drifts off each muscle belly as glowing embers, brighter and denser the harder that muscle is working. These are the same literature-grounded curves as the Explorer page, drawn as forces acting on things instead of lines on a graph.
